A first moodboard for the redesign: a hand-drawn road you travel and zoom along, by day on paper and by lamplight at night. Try the camera below.

Horizon

Scenery moves at different speeds as the camera zooms; the road, flags and tags stay put. At night, look up: the arc of seven stars is Corona Borealis, the crown the myth sets in the sky for Ariadne. Press Esc to zoom out.

How it should feel

A field notebook you walk through with someone who has read the whole thing. Warm and unhurried, hand-made but not childish.

A field notebook, not a dashboard

Pencil, paper and margin notes. Nothing turns into a chart unless it has to.

Lamplit, not neon

Night is the same road after dark, lit by one small gold light. Deep indigo, kept dark and quiet.

Solid is you, dashed is maybe

Solid lines come from your files. Dashed lines are possibilities or things not stated.

Unhurried

Camera moves take about three quarters of a second and ease in and out. Nothing bounces.

Guided, not gamified

No streaks, scores or confetti. The coach speaks first and asks one question at a time.

Colour by day and by night

Both themes side by side, whatever the page is set to. Hex values and contrast ratios are read live from the tokens, so they cannot drift from what you see.

Line and paper

Brown sketch lines do most of the work. Everything is drawn once, with a fixed wobble, and never redrawn while it moves.

Outline

Walnut, 2 px, a little wobble. Round corners at 12 px.

Hatch

Thin walnut strokes at an angle. Far hills are looser than near ones.

Contour

Closed loops, quiet, only as texture behind the road.

Thread

The road and the timeline. Orange, 3 px, with a fine brown shadow line.

Future route

Dashed, nine on and seven off. Used for "Where next" and anything not yet known.

Paper grain

A faint fractal-noise overlay, lighter and fainter at night. Already in the app.

  • Same seed, same wobble: an element looks the same every visit, and is redrawn only when its size changes.
  • Three line weights only: 1, 2 and 3 px. Nothing is perfectly straight.
  • Solid means it came from your files. Dashed means possibility. This rule holds everywhere, including empty states.

Type

Keep the two families the app already uses. They are distinct, accessible and already loaded.

Keep and avoid

Keep

  • Paper, walnut pencil, one orange thread.
  • Hand-drawn frames, drawn once per size.
  • Gaegu for map lettering, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading.
  • A coach that speaks first and asks one question at a time.
  • Honest labels: "Not stated" beats a guess.

Avoid

  • Bright purple or neon accents. Night stays a deep, quiet indigo.
  • Decorative gradients, glass blur, heavy shadows.
  • Stock illustrated people, emoji icons, sparkle icons on buttons or headings.
  • Streaks, scores, confetti.
  • Scroll-jacking, and parallax on text.
